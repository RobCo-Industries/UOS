 Starting a pump
 1. Confirm the suction valve is fully open. The stem shows a green band
    when it is open.
 2. Confirm the discharge valve is closed to one quarter.
 3. Select the pump on the control panel and press START once.
 4. Watch the discharge pressure. It should rise within five seconds.
    If it does not, press STOP and call the duty engineer.
 5. Open the discharge valve slowly over one minute.
 6. Record the start time and the pressure in the station log.
