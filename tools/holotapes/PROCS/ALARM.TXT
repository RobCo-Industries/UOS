When the alarm horn sounds, press ACKNOWLEDGE first. This silences the
horn but keeps the alarm lamp lit.

Read the alarm text on the panel and find it in the alarm binder. Each
alarm has one page with its likely causes in order. Work down the page.

If the cause is not on the page, or the alarm returns within an hour,
call the duty engineer. Write every alarm in the station log, even
those that clear by themselves.
