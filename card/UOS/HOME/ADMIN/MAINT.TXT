MAINTENANCE NOTES

Pump two
	Bearing noise since spring. Grease every Friday until the new
	bearing arrives. Do not run it above three quarters speed.

Yard lights
	Lamp six flickers. The spare is on the top shelf of the store room.
	Switch the lights off at the panel before you change a lamp.

North door
	The closer is slow in cold weather. Give it ten seconds before you
	report it stuck.

Store room lock
	The lock fails safe: it opens when the power goes. Check it after
	every power cut.
