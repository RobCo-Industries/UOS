Monday: intake screen
Arrived at the Alder Creek pump station at seven. The night shift left
a note that pump two sounded rough after midnight. I listened for ten
minutes and heard nothing unusual, so I checked the intake screen
instead.

The screen was half covered in leaves from the storm on Saturday. I
raked it clear and logged the flow before and after. Flow rose from 410
to 455 litres a second. Pump two settled at once.

Note for the next shift: check the screen after any wind, not only
after rain.
